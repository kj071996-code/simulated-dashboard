How to Read This

Expand▾

One-line definitions for the main measures. All figures are simulated and worked out on this page from the rows above. Dates show as Mon YYYY (e.g., Nov 2025). Units are $, hrs, and % throughout. Percentages use one decimal.

Win Rate
The share of bids we won in the selected period.
Cost per Bid
What we spend, on average, to prepare and submit one bid, shown with AI help and by hand.
Hours Returned
Office hours the AI tool gives back in the period, from finding work, intake and AI-prepared briefs, and paperwork.
Estimate Accuracy (Won Jobs)
The share of won jobs whose actual cost landed within ±10% of the estimate.
Field Hours
Electrician hours logged on jobs in the period. The hours for each job add up to that job's actual hours.
Material Spend
The actual cost of materials bought for jobs in the period, with the estimate shown next to it.
Average Miss
How far actual cost landed from the estimate, on average, across won jobs. Negative means the estimate was too high.
Price & Margin (Simulated)
Price is a simulated field: the estimate marked up by a set simulated percentage. Margin is price minus actual cost, for won jobs only.
Win Rate for the Jobs Shown Here
Won jobs divided by all the sample jobs in the tables, by count and by estimated dollars. It differs from the overall Win Rate above, which counts every bid in the period.
AI-Prepared Brief
A short job summary the AI tool prepares before we bid. Jobs with one are compared with jobs without one.
